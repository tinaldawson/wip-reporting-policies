
# Connect or Reconnect WIP Reporting

WIP Reporting is a private financial reporting application operated locally by an authorized administrator.

To connect or reconnect a QuickBooks Online company:

1. Open the Intuit Developer Portal.
2. Select the WIP Reporting application.
3. Use the authorized OAuth connection process to grant access to the appropriate QuickBooks Online company.
4. Return to the local reporting application to complete the connection.

For assistance, contact the WIP Reporting administrator.
