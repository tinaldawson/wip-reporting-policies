# WIP Reporting — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 12, 2026

### 1. Introduction

WIP Reporting is a private financial reporting application developed for internal business use.

This Privacy Policy explains how information obtained from connected QuickBooks Online accounts and other authorized business systems is accessed, processed, stored, and protected.

### 2. Information Accessed

With authorization, WIP Reporting may retrieve business and accounting information, including:

- Customer and supplier information
- Invoices, credit memos, bills, and purchases
- Journal entries and accounting classifications
- Projects, classes, locations, and custom fields
- Transaction dates, descriptions, and financial amounts
- Other accounting information necessary for authorized reporting

Some records may contain personal information, including names and contact information.

### 3. Purpose of Data Processing

Information is accessed and processed exclusively for authorized business purposes, including:

- Work-in-progress reporting
- Project cost and profitability analysis
- Financial reconciliation
- Management reporting
- Internal accounting analysis and operational planning

Information is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

### 4. Authorization and Access

Connections to QuickBooks Online are established using Intuit's OAuth 2.0 authorization process.

WIP Reporting does not require users to disclose their QuickBooks passwords to the application.

Only authorized QuickBooks Online companies and approved personnel may access the reporting system.

### 5. Data Storage and Security

Financial information may be processed and stored in locally managed reporting files, databases, or other approved business systems.

Appropriate safeguards will be maintained to protect confidential information, including access controls and secure handling of application credentials and authorization tokens.

Access to stored information is limited to authorized individuals.

### 6. Data Sharing

Accounting information is not sold or disclosed for advertising purposes.

Information may be shared with authorized personnel or approved service providers only as necessary for legitimate business operations and subject to applicable confidentiality, security, and legal requirements.

### 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as necessary for authorized business reporting, operational requirements, and applicable legal or accounting recordkeeping obligations.

When a QuickBooks Online company revokes authorization, the application will discontinue access to that company's data.

Stored information will be securely deleted when required, subject to applicable legal recordkeeping obligations.

### 8. Third-Party Services

WIP Reporting integrates with QuickBooks Online, a service operated by Intuit Inc.

Information processed directly by Intuit is subject to Intuit's own privacy practices and applicable agreements.
