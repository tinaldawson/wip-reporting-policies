# WIP Reporting — Terms & Conditions

**Effective Date:** November 12, 2026

### 1. Purpose

WIP Reporting is a private, internally developed financial reporting application designed to retrieve and analyze accounting information from authorized QuickBooks Online companies.

The application supports work-in-progress reporting, project profitability analysis, financial reconciliation, and internal management reporting.

### 2. Authorized Use

WIP Reporting is intended exclusively for authorized personnel of participating businesses.

The application is not offered for public subscription, commercial distribution, or use by unauthorized third parties.

Access to financial information is restricted to individuals who have been granted appropriate authorization.

### 3. QuickBooks Online Integration

WIP Reporting connects to QuickBooks Online using Intuit's authorized application programming interface (API) and OAuth 2.0 authorization.

Only QuickBooks Online companies that have explicitly authorized the application may be accessed.

The application is intended to retrieve accounting information for reporting and analysis. It is not intended to modify accounting records unless additional functionality is expressly developed and authorized.

### 4. Accuracy of Financial Information

Reports generated by WIP Reporting are based on information retrieved from connected accounting systems and other authorized business data sources.

Users are responsible for reviewing and reconciling reports before relying on them for accounting, financial reporting, taxation, or management decisions.

WIP Reporting does not replace professional accounting judgment or established financial controls.

### 5. Confidentiality

Information accessed through WIP Reporting is confidential business information and must be handled in accordance with applicable company policies and legal requirements.

Unauthorized disclosure, distribution, or use of financial information is prohibited.

### 6. Availability and Changes

The application may be modified, updated, restricted, or discontinued at the discretion of its operator.

No guarantee is made that the application will operate without interruption or error.

### 7. Termination of Access

Access may be revoked when an individual is no longer authorized to use the application or when a participating business disconnects its QuickBooks Online account.
